# Bot audit (2026-10-29, owner's strategy brief §10 and §12.1)

## What the bot does today (v0.5)
For each of six assets (GLD, SLV, GDX, NEM, AEM, WPM), after each US close, it sets a target holding of
`min(1, 15% / forecast volatility)` of the asset's sleeve (sleeve = 1/6 of the account), where forecast volatility is the
21-day realised volatility scaled by gold's implied/realised volatility ratio (GVZ). It is always invested, never
borrows, and only trades when an asset is more than 5 points (of its sleeve) away from target. The ML model,
technical signals, news and fundamentals are shown as context but do not trade.

## Strongest components
1. **One rule everywhere** (D-005): the same functions size the backtest, the dashboard, the bot and the paper trader;
   tests check that live decisions equal backtested ones.
2. **Honest testing**: completed bars only, publication lags, next-open fills checked against next-close fills, costs
   at 1× and 2×, cash interest, pre-registered experiments, bootstrap, deflated Sharpe and PBO, an untouched-then-
   reused holdout plus a pre-2013 period and eight other assets for confirmation.
3. **Risk sizing that works**: about half the worst fall of holding across 20 years and five stress windows.
4. **A record of every idea and failure** (49-row transfer matrix, 30 experiment files, 29 decisions).

## Weaknesses found (with evidence) and what was done
| # | Weakness | Evidence | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Backtest held each asset in its own pot; the paper trader sizes against the whole account | Code review: `combine_sleeves` vs `paper.rebalance` | One-account simulator built; difference ≤ 0.04 Sharpe (EXP-035, D-028) |
| 2 | No guard against duplicate orders (the bot loops every 60 s) | Code review: `run_bot.py` | One order per asset per day, max orders per day (`safety.py`), tested |
| 3 | No off switch, no bad-price guard | Code review | `live.trading_enabled`, `STOP_TRADING` file, 25% price-jump guard, tested |
| 4 | Decisions not explained in a structured, stored form | Brief §10 | JSON-lines decision record per planned or blocked order (signal, size, risk, exit rule, every check) |
| 5 | Monitoring lacked drawdown, exposure, cost share, unrealised P&L | Code review: `app.py` | Added to the paper tab, with decision records and the off-switch state |
| 6 | Trading depends on the slow ML model succeeding each morning, although ML no longer trades | `run_bot.py`: a failed `research()` skips the ticker for the day | **Open**: to be removed in the Alpaca build (size directly from prices and GVZ) |
| 7 | Paper fills use the last price seen, not the next open | `paper.py` docstring | **Open**: the Alpaca connection will use opening-auction orders (D-023) and record fill vs expected price |
| 8 | Survivorship: the asset list was chosen in 2026 | Data limitation | Partly addressed by eight other assets (EXP-021); delisted miners not available free |
| 9 | Return per unit of risk is no better than holding | EXP-020, EXP-034 | Stated plainly in every report; the bot's value is smaller falls |
