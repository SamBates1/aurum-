# New ideas review (2026-10-29)

Status: short literature scan after the first experiment run (D-017), which found Aurum's value is risk
control, not return prediction. So this scan favours ideas that improve risk estimates, execution realism
and turnover over new direction forecasts. Sources S19–S24 in [../sources.md](../sources.md).

| # | Idea | What the evidence says | Fit for Aurum | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Check fills at the next close instead of the next open** | Quantpedia [S20]: in daily OHLC data the "open" is the first print, often tiny volume and not executable; closes match the closing auction. For GDX an OHLC overnight strategy showed ~30%/yr, falling to ~8.6%/yr with a 09:31 fill from minute data | **Direct risk to Aurum's backtest**, which fills every trade at the OHLC open | **EXP-010** robustness check: identical strategy filled at the next close (market-on-close). Required before trusting any result |
| 2 | **Better volatility forecasts for sizing (HAR model)** | Volatility is far more predictable than returns. Comparing many models out of sample, the HAR benchmark (daily + weekly + monthly realised volatility) is "hard to significantly and unambiguously beat" [S19] | Aurum sizes positions with a plain 21-day volatility; its edge is risk control, so better risk estimates go straight to the edge | **EXP-011**: size on HAR (and EWMA) forecasts vs 21-day vol; judge on realised-vs-target volatility and drawdown |
| 3 | **Continuous, smoother trend signal** | Baltas & Kosowski [S21]: more efficient volatility estimators and trend detection cut time-series-momentum turnover by over a third with no significant performance loss, after costs | Aurum trades in/out (BUY/SELL) and paid ~0.7%/yr in costs in development | **EXP-012**: scale exposure by signal strength with a no-trade band instead of all-or-nothing |
| 4 | **Gold implied volatility (CBOE GVZ)** | Market's forward-looking gold volatility; daily on Yahoo (`^GVZ`) and FRED. Little peer-reviewed evidence it predicts gold *returns* [S22 is descriptive] | Better input for sizing/regimes than VIX, which is equity volatility | Add as a feature for #2 and as a gate replacing VIX in a re-run of EXP-003 |
| 5 | **Prediction intervals (conformal / quantile)** | Rolling conformal intervals achieve near-nominal coverage on gold (94.3% vs 95% target), but simple volatility-model intervals scored better in most comparisons [S23] | Fits the owner's **trade-plan idea**: give a likely price *range* and stop distance instead of a direction | Build ranges from the #2 volatility forecast; check coverage; use for trade-plan stops/targets |
| 6 | **Gold seasonality (September, November)** | Baur reports strong, significant September (~2.2%) and November (~1.8%) returns, robust to controls [S24]; but calendar effects are a classic data-mining risk and the summary is internally inconsistent about units | Cheap to test, weak prior | Low priority. If tested, count it as a trial in the DSR |
| 7 | **GDX overnight drift strategy** | Large in OHLC data, mostly an artefact of unrealistic open fills [S20] | – | **Reject** as a strategy; it is the reason for #1 |

## Suggested order
1. EXP-010 (validity of every result so far), 2. EXP-011, 3. EXP-012, 4. GVZ, 5. trade-plan ranges.
All are pre-registered before running, use the same acceptance rules, and add to the trial count.
